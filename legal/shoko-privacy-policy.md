# Shoko Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what data the Shoko Discord bot ("Shoko") collects and how you can ask for it to be deleted. Shoko is run by 08, Discord `meatforlove` ("I", "me").

## 1. What Shoko stores

Shoko stores very little. It only stores:

- **Bot settings:** the bot's status and activity text, which voice channel it should stay in (and its mute or deafen choice), and Auto-React settings for each server (which channels or messages get reactions, which emoji, and counts).
- **Discord IDs:** server IDs, channel IDs, and emoji IDs that are part of those settings.
- **Technical status:** whether the bot is online, and results of commands sent from the control panel.

Shoko does **not** store message text, user profiles, or direct messages. It does not record, store, or play voice audio.

## 2. What Shoko reads

To use Auto-React, Shoko reads messages in the servers and channels where it has permission to see them. It uses a message only to decide whether to add a reaction. It does not save the message content.

## 3. How data is used

I use the data only to run Shoko's features and to find and fix bugs. I do not sell data and do not use it for ads.

## 4. Where data is kept

- Settings are stored in a database on Cloudflare (D1).
- The bot runs on a hosting service (Wispbyte).
- The private control panel runs on Cloudflare. Only the owner can sign in.

These services process the data for me. I do not share data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

## 5. How long data is kept

Settings are kept while Shoko is in use. If Shoko is removed from a server, or if you ask, I will delete that server's settings as soon as I reasonably can. Some data may stay in backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

## 6. Your choices

You can ask me to tell you what data Shoko has about your server, or to delete it. Contact me on Discord (`meatforlove`). I may need to confirm you are the server owner or an admin. You can also stop all collection by removing Shoko from your server.

## 7. Children

Shoko is for people who are old enough to use Discord (13 or older). I do not knowingly keep data from anyone under that age.

## 8. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect data. Access to the control panel is limited to the owner, and the bot connects to it with a secret key. No system is fully secure, so I cannot promise that data will never be exposed.

## 9. Changes

I may change this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when I do.
